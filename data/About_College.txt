SHANMUGA INDUSTRIES ARTS AND SCIENCE COLLEGE (SIASC)

ABOUT THE COLLEGE

Shanmuga Industries Arts and Science College (SIASC) is a self-financing institution located on Manalurpet Road, Tiruvannamalai, Tamil Nadu. The college was established in 1996 with the vision of providing quality higher education to students from rural and urban backgrounds.

The institution is affiliated with Thiruvalluvar University and offers a wide range of Undergraduate and Postgraduate programmes in Arts, Science, Commerce, Computer Applications, and Management. The college is committed to academic excellence, innovation, discipline, research, and the holistic development of students.

SIASC provides a student-friendly learning environment with experienced faculty members, modern classrooms, well-equipped laboratories, a rich library, computer labs, Wi-Fi facilities, placement support, hostel facilities, transport services, sports, NSS, and various co-curricular and extracurricular activities.

The college focuses on developing students with strong technical knowledge, communication skills, leadership qualities, ethical values, and social responsibility. Through continuous learning, practical exposure, seminars, workshops, industrial visits, and placement training, students are prepared for higher education, entrepreneurship, and successful careers.
