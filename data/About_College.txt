SHANMUGA INDUSTRIES ARTS AND SCIENCE COLLEGE

ABOUT THE COLLEGE

Shanmuga Industries Arts and Science College (SIASC) is located in Tiruvannamalai District, Tamil Nadu.

The college was established in 1996.

The institution is affiliated with Thiruvalluvar University.

Courses Offered:
- B.Sc Data Science
- B.Sc Computer Science
- BCA
- B.Com
- BBA
- BA English
- BA Tamil

Facilities:
- Library
- Computer Lab
- Hostel
- Transport
- Placement Cell
- Sports
- Wi-Fi Campus
