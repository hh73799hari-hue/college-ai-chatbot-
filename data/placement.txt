SHANMUGA INDUSTRIES ARTS AND SCIENCE COLLEGE

PLACEMENT CELL

The Placement Cell of Shanmuga Industries Arts and Science College is committed to preparing students for successful careers by improving their technical knowledge, communication skills, and employability.

OBJECTIVES

• To improve students' employability skills.
• To organize campus recruitment drives.
• To provide career guidance and counselling.
• To conduct placement training programmes.
• To develop students' aptitude and soft skills.
• To build strong industry interaction.

PLACEMENT ACTIVITIES

• Campus Recruitment Drives
• Career Guidance Programmes
• Aptitude Training
• Soft Skills Development
• Communication Skills Training
• Mock Interviews
• Resume Preparation
• Group Discussion Practice
• Personality Development Programmes
• Industry Interaction Sessions

TRAINING PROVIDED

Students are encouraged to participate in various training programmes that improve their confidence and prepare them for recruitment processes.

The placement training includes:

• Aptitude Tests
• Logical Reasoning
• Quantitative Aptitude
• Verbal Ability
• Interview Skills
• Technical Skills
• Communication Skills

CAREER SUPPORT

The Placement Cell supports students by providing information about employment opportunities, higher education guidance, and career development programmes.

VISION

To prepare students with the skills, confidence, and professional attitude required to achieve successful careers.

MISSION

To connect students with employment opportunities through continuous training, industry interaction, and career guidance.
