SHANMUGA INDUSTRIES ARTS AND SCIENCE COLLEGE

Question: What is the name of the college?
Answer: The name of the institution is Shanmuga Industries Arts and Science College (SIASC).

Question: Where is the college located?
Answer: The college is located on Manalurpet Road, Tiruvannamalai, Tamil Nadu.

Question: Which university is the college affiliated with?
Answer: The college is affiliated with Thiruvalluvar University.

Question: When was the college established?
Answer: The college was established in 1996.

Question: Does the college offer B.Sc. Data Science?
Answer: Yes. The college offers B.Sc. Data Science.

Question: Does the college offer BCA?
Answer: Yes. The college offers Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA).

Question: Does the college offer B.Com?
Answer: Yes. The college offers B.Com, B.Com Computer Applications, and B.Com Finance & Accounting.

Question: What UG courses are available?
Answer: The college offers various UG courses including B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., BBA, and BCA programmes.

Question: Does the college provide hostel facilities?
Answer: Yes. Hostel facilities are available for students.

Question: Is transport available?
Answer: Yes. Transport facilities are available from nearby locations.

Question: Is Wi-Fi available on campus?
Answer: Yes. Wi-Fi facilities are available on campus.
