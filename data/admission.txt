SHANMUGA INDUSTRIES ARTS AND SCIENCE COLLEGE

ADMISSION

The college offers admission to eligible students for various Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) programmes.

ADMISSION PROCESS

• Students can obtain the admission application from the college admission office or through the official college website.

• Applicants should fill in the application form with correct personal and academic details.

• Admission is granted based on the eligibility criteria prescribed by Thiruvalluvar University and the Government of Tamil Nadu.

ELIGIBILITY

UNDERGRADUATE (UG)

• Candidates must have successfully completed Higher Secondary Examination (10+2) or its equivalent from a recognized board.

POSTGRADUATE (PG)

• Candidates must possess a Bachelor's Degree in the relevant discipline from a recognized university.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

Applicants should submit the following documents during admission:

• 10th Mark Sheet
• 12th Mark Sheet
• Transfer Certificate (TC)
• Conduct Certificate
• Community Certificate (if applicable)
• Aadhaar Card Copy
• Passport Size Photographs
• Degree Mark Sheets (For PG Admission)

ADMISSION GUIDELINES

• Candidates should ensure that all information provided is accurate.

• Original certificates should be produced for verification during admission.

• Admission is confirmed only after successful verification of documents and payment of the prescribed fees.

SCHOLARSHIPS

Eligible students may apply for Government Scholarships and other scholarship schemes as per the prevailing rules and regulations.
